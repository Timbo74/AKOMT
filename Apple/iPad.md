# iPad
## Troubleshooting
### iPad Pro won't power on

**Perform a Force Restart**  For iPad Pro models without a Home button, press and quickly release the `Volume Up button`, then press and quickly release the `Volume Down button`, and finally press and hold the `Top (Power) button` until the Apple logo appears.  If your model has a Home button, hold both the `Top button` and `Home butto`n until the logo appears.
