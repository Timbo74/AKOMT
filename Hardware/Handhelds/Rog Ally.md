# BIOS

## Enter the Bios
### Handheld Mode
1. Power Down Completely: Turn off your ROG Ally completely. Do not leave it in sleep or hibernation mode. You can do this by opening the menu (or Command Center) and selecting Shut Down. Wait until the screen turns black and the joystick lights go out.
2. Press the `Power button` to turn the device on.
3. Repeatedly Press `Volume Down`: Immediately start tapping or spamming the `Volume Down` (-) button continuously as the device begins to boot up.
4. Enter BIOS: Keep tapping the `Volume Down` button until the BIOS/UEFI configuration screen appears.
### Desktop Mode Using a Keyboard
When the device is completely shut down, persistently hold the ```Esc``` key on the keyboard and simultaneously press the ```Power button``` to boot up. Once the Boot menu screen appears, you may release the ```Esc``` key.

When the device is completely shut down, hold the ```F2``` key on the keyboard and simultaneously press the ```Power button``` to boot up.  Once the BIOS configuration screen appears, you may release the ```F2``` key.
